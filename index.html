High mucus plug burden was defined as an 18-segment mucus plug score ≥4.

Model equations

Probability = 1 / (1 + exp(-LP)). ED uses ln(1 + patient-days). Female and current smoking are coded as 1; male and other smoking categories as 0. Heart rate and MMEF use raw units.

Development cohort: 458 adults hospitalized with asthma · Beijing Tongren Hospital, Capital Medical University.

Research use only. These models were developed and internally evaluated in a single-center retrospective cohort of adults hospitalized with asthma. They have not undergone independent external validation. Estimated probabilities should not be used as a substitute for clinically indicated CT or as a stand-alone basis for treatment decisions.

No patient data are transmitted or stored.
