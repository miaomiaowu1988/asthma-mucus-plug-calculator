Model equation
Clinical model LP = -2.960 + 1.161 × ln(1 + ED) + 1.132 × NasalPolyps + 0.315 × GINA - 0.565 × Female + 0.624 × BPD + 0.836 × CurrentSmoking Clinical model with MMEF LP = -0.411 + 1.292 × ln(1 + ED) + 1.256 × NasalPolyps + 0.175 × GINA - 0.764 × Female + 0.441 × BPD + 0.681 × CurrentSmoking - 0.401 × (MMEF / 10)

Probability = 1 / (1 + exp(-LP)). Smoking is coded as Current = 1; Never, Former, and Indeterminate = 0.

Development cohort: 458 adults hospitalized with asthma · Beijing Tongren Hospital, Capital Medical University.
